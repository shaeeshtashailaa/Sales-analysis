# ElectroHub Sales Data Analysis – Power BI

## Project Overview

**ElectroHub** is an interactive Power BI sales analytics dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, quantity sold, discounts, orders, products, customers, and city-level sales.

The dashboard was developed from a defined set of business requirements and provides interactive filtering to help users explore sales performance from different perspectives.

## Business Requirements

The project addresses the following requirements:

1. Identify the **Top 5 and Bottom 5 products** by Sales, Profit, and Quantity Sold.
2. Analyze sales trends over **daily, monthly, quarterly, and annual** periods.
3. Show the relationship between **Sales and Profit**.
4. Compare **Sales, Profit, and Quantity Sold** between two user-selected periods.
5. Analyze the **average discount** offered across discount categories.
6. Display the **total number of orders**.
7. Provide order-level details including Sales, Profit, Discount, Net Sales, and other fields with visual filtering by Product, Date, Customer ID, and Promotion Category.
8. Analyze **sales by city**.

## Key Analysis Areas

### Product Performance
Identifies products contributing the highest and lowest sales, profit, and quantity sold.

### Sales Trends
Supports time-based analysis of sales performance across different periods.

### Profitability
Uses the relationship between Profit and Net Sales to understand financial performance.

### Period Comparison
Allows users to compare sales, profit, and quantity sold across selected periods.

### Discount Analysis
Examines average discount levels across discount categories.

### Order Analysis
Provides total order information and detailed order-level analysis through filters.

### Geographic Analysis
Shows sales distribution across different cities.
